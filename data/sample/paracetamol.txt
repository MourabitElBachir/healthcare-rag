Paracetamol, also called acetaminophen in some countries, is an ingredient used in medicines for pain or fever. Products may have different strengths, formulations, and instructions. This illustrative document deliberately gives no dose, dosing interval, or personal treatment plan. Follow the product information and advice from a qualified professional who can consider the person's situation.

Vérifier le nom des substances actives dans tous les médicaments utilisés. Certains produits contre le rhume ou la douleur peuvent contenir la même substance sous des présentations différentes. Ne pas supposer que deux noms commerciaux correspondent à deux substances distinctes. En cas de doute sur une association, demander conseil avant de prendre les produits ensemble.

A pharmacist or clinician can review other medicines, existing conditions, allergies, and individual circumstances. People with questions about liver problems, alcohol use, pregnancy, or ongoing treatment should seek professional advice. An adult information sheet should not be used to decide how to give medicine to a child. Instructions can differ between people and between products.
